Playing online

When you create or join an online game, your browser connects directly to the other player's browser. To set that up, your browser contacts a third-party matchmaking service (PeerJS), and if a direct connection is not possible the game data passes through that service's relay servers. This means your IP address is visible to that service and to the player you connect with. We do not record or store any of it.
