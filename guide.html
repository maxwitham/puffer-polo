How to play Puffer Polo

Puffer Polo is underwater polo for two octopuses. The ball is a pufferfish who would rather be left alone. Knock him into the other octopus's cave more times than they knock him into yours.

Play now

The basics

The first octopus to score three goals wins the match. Coral defends the cave on the left and Violet defends the cave on the right. Every time the ball goes all the way into a cave, the other side scores and play restarts from the middle.

The puffer drifts back toward the middle of the pool by himself, so he never gets stuck on the sand. That also means a slow shot aimed high or low will curve back toward cave height.

Chest run

Chest run is the single-player ladder. There are eight opponents, each tougher than the last, and each with its own pool and ball. Beat one and you win a chest and unlock the next opponent. You can replay any opponent you have reached as often as you like, and every win gives another chest.

Pools and balls

  • Coral Reef and The Abyss have two round bumpers in the middle, and Shipwreck has four. Shots bounce off them, and so do you.
  • Ice Cavern is slippery, so you are slow to get going and slow to stop, and its caves are smaller.
  • The Abyss has the biggest caves, so goals come quickly at both ends.
  • The beach ball is big and floats upward. The pearl is small, fast and very bouncy. The jellyfish is slow and kicks itself upward every second or so. The sea urchin is heavy, so nudging it barely moves it and you need a dash.

Chests and skins

There are two ways to get a chest. Beating a Chest run opponent always gives you one. Winning a quick match gives you a chance of finding one. Chests are only ever won by playing. They cannot be bought.

A chest you win is kept under Chests on the menu until you open it, so you can open it straight away or save a few up. Press Open and the chest rattles, then the lid comes up and you see what was inside.

Most chests hold one skin for your octopus, and each skin comes with stat bonuses. Some chests are empty. If a chest gives you a skin you already own, that is a duplicate and you get nothing new. There are 34 skins to collect in five rarities: Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary and two Mythic skins, Rainbow Royal and Celestial. Open the Skins screen from the menu to see your collection and choose which one to wear. Your skin also shows when you play online.

These are the chances, in percent, of what each chest holds. Within a rarity, every skin is equally likely. Legendary chests are never empty.

Controls

  • One player or online: swim with W A S D or the arrow keys, and dash with Space or Shift.
  • Two players on one keyboard: Coral uses W A S D and Space. Violet uses the arrow keys and Enter or /.
  • Phone or tablet: drag the joystick to swim and tap the Dash button. Turn a phone sideways and the pool fills the screen, with the joystick and Dash button on top of it.
  • P pauses a match against the computer or on a shared keyboard.

Dashing

Swimming into the puffer only nudges him. A dash is your real shot: it launches him at about twice the speed of a nudge, in the direction from your octopus through the puffer. Line the three of you up, octopus, puffer, cave, and then dash.

You hold three dashes, shown as dots under your octopus. Each one takes about a second and a half to come back, so three dashes in a row leaves you slow for a while. Keep one in reserve for defending.

Dashing into the other octopus when they are not dashing stuns them for half a second and knocks them away. It is the quickest way to clear a defender out of a cave mouth.

Skin bonuses

Every skin except the starter adds a permanent bonus to one or more of four stats, and rarer skins add more. The bonuses apply in every mode, including online.

  • Speed: how fast you swim.
  • Strength: how hard a dash sends the ball.
  • Dash refill: how quickly a used dash comes back.
  • Boost time: how long bubble power-ups last for you, including how long a Zap stuns your opponent.

Power-up bubbles

Every so often a bubble rises from the sand. Touch it to use it straight away. If nobody takes it, it pops at the surface.

  • Plus sign, Giant: you grow for seven seconds. You are heavier, harder to push, and your dash shots are faster.
  • Chevrons, Turbo: six seconds of faster swimming and dashes that never run out.
  • Bolt, Zap: the other octopus is stunned for a second and a half. Take the shot while they cannot move.

Tips

  • Get behind the puffer first. Hitting him from the side sends him into a wall. Swim round to your own side of him before you dash.
  • Use the corners. The pool's slanted corners bounce the puffer back toward the middle, so a missed shot often comes straight back out.
  • Shoot past the defender. A defender sitting in the middle of a cave blocks a straight shot. Aim for the top or bottom of the cave mouth, or stun them first.
  • Do not chase every bubble. Leaving your cave to grab one gives the other side an open goal. Go only when the puffer is far from your cave.
  • Defend with your body. Staying between the puffer and your cave beats dashing at him and missing.

Playing online with a friend

Choose Online on the menu and press Create game. You get a four-letter code. Send it to your friend, or press Copy invite link and send the link. They choose Online, type the code and press Join, and the match starts for both of you. The player who created the game is Coral and the player who joined is Violet.

The two browsers connect directly to each other, so there is nothing to install and no account to make. If the player who created the game closes or hides their tab, the match stops for both players.

Quick match

Quick match pairs you with a random player. Choose Quick match on the menu and press Find a match. If somebody else is looking at the same time, the match starts within a few seconds in the standard pool. The game is new, so there will often be nobody else looking. You can keep waiting, or press Play the computer for a match straight away. Keep the game's tab on screen while you wait, because a hidden tab stops searching.

Win a quick match and you might find a chest. Against another player you find one about two wins in three, and it can be any type, though mostly Common or Rare. Against the computer it is about one win in three, and never a Legendary chest. The computer gets harder as you climb the Chest run ladder.
