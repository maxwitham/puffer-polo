How to play

The aim

Knock the ball into the other octopus's cave. The first to three goals wins.

Controls

W A S D or the arrow keys swim, Space or Shift dashes. On a phone, use the joystick and the dash button.

Dash

A dash hits the ball far harder and stuns the other octopus. You hold three, shown as dots under you, and each takes a moment to refill.

Bubbles

Pop one for a power-up. The plus makes you giant, the chevrons give endless dashes, the bolt zaps your rival.

Chests and skins

Beat a ladder opponent to win a chest, or find one by winning a quick match. Open them from Chests on the menu. Most hold a skin, some are empty, and a duplicate skin gives nothing.

Skin bonuses

Skins are more than a look. Each one adds to your speed, strength, dash refill or boost time, and rarer skins add more. The Skins screen shows what each gives.

Online

Quick match pairs you with a random player who is looking at the same time. To play a friend, choose Online and press Create game, then send them the four-letter code.
