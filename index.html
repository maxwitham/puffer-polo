Controls

W A S D or the arrow keys swim, Space or Shift dashes. On a phone, use the joystick and the dash button.

Two on one keyboard

Coral uses W A S D and Space. Violet uses the arrow keys and Enter or /.

Online

Choose Online and press Create game, then send your friend the four-letter code. They choose Online, type it in and press Join.

Dash

A dash hits the puffer far harder and stuns the other octopus. You hold three, shown as dots under you, and each takes a moment to refill.

Bubbles

Pop one for a power-up. The plus makes you giant, the chevrons give endless dashes, the bolt zaps your rival.

Match

Puffer Polo is a free two-player browser game. Matches last two minutes; if the score is level, the next goal wins.
